# ISLA V2.22 — upgrade rebalance

The starting repository was main commit a1a30b1. All 113 required V2.21 sources matched their manifest. This patch preserves existing ownership, currencies, purchased upgrades and prices. Already-owned upgrades receive these stats automatically, with no new purchase.

## Across all 19 cars

Normal nitro speed and engine power are now +25% at Level 2 and +50% at Level 3, relative to Level 1. Speeds are rounded to whole km/h. Level 1 stays unchanged. Most Level 2 cars get 6–8 seconds of nitro with a six-second refill; most Level 3 cars get 8–10 seconds with a five-second refill. Some cars have stronger specialized nitro settings, shown below.

The upgrade screen explicitly shows speed and engine gains over Level 1. Engine power is a model parameter; actual acceleration also depends on mass, surface and speed.

## Consistent effects

Fire Truck pressure changes now flatten the water arc enough to reach the upgraded 32/42-meter ranges over flat ground. Rendering and collision checks share the same arc; buildings and raised terrain still block water. Launcher reward validation follows the active level's lower launch threshold, so valid 90 km/h Level 3 launches can earn Aura. Water Skidder's Level 3 surge now lasts 2.5 seconds with stronger acceleration and an eight-second cooldown. Nuker Level 3 targeting reaches 110 meters ahead, while its manual cooldown remains five seconds.

## Trails and joystick follow-up

Colored trails now use soft-edged mesh ribbons, nominally 0.36 meters wide for a standard car and scaled/clamped to 0.20–0.65 meters for other car sizes. Initial opacity rises from 0.34 to 0.72. The selected color and ten-second fade remain. Geometry is a fixed-size circular buffer, and airborne travel, pauses and teleports do not create long connecting strips. Existing black handbrake marks are unchanged.

The joystick moves **30 pixels up and 40 pixels right relative to the first V2.22 patch**. Its normal offset from the original control is now 106 pixels right / 72 pixels up; short-landscape offset is 94 right / 58 up. Narrow-phone clearance rules remain in effect. On portrait phones, speed meters and pedal buttons reflow so the relocated stick does not cover them.

Non-passive touch-start, touch-move and Safari gesture handlers suppress default zoom gestures on the joystick during play, in addition to touch-action:none and double-click prevention. These are scoped to the joystick. Physical iPad Safari behavior still needs a device check; browser-toolbar gestures are outside page control.

## Validation

The initial V2.22 full suite passed 216 tests. After this follow-up, all 27 targeted gameplay/input tests passed, including a new ribbon geometry/lifetime/discontinuity check and joystick gesture suppression coverage. The follow-up Worker dry-run and source checks passed.

Targeted checks cover all 57 normal nitro speed limits, actual acceleration improvement, nitro duration/recharge, flap budgets and ground takeoff, jump/dash/lift abilities, stronger rocket timing, hose range and collision, distant auto-aim, and server rewards at all Launcher thresholds.

Browser layout checks used the actual HUD styles/modules at 320×568, 390×844, 844×390 and 1180×820, plus the mobile upgrade screen showing Flappy Level 2 at 335 km/h, +25% speed/engine and 30 flaps. Temporary fixtures and sample balances are excluded from the patch. Full WebGL driving and physical Safari multi-touch remain device checks.

The one-time test credit is unchanged and will not repeat. Water Skidder retains its 30-second budget and ten-second land recharge. Plane Car Aura-farming changes remain outside this update.
