ISLA V2.41

Grappler
- Press F / GRAPPLE to attach to the nearest reachable building surface,
  including buildings beside or behind the car. No aiming cone is required.
- Stronger pull and faster steering around the anchor. The car aligns with
  its movement. Brake to slow the swing, accelerate to reel in faster, and
  press F / GRAPPLE again to release with your momentum.
- Existing upgrade reach, prices and nitro speeds stay the same.

Inferno
- Stone trails are gray-brown, with the same shared support and lifetime.
- Removed the ring around Inferno while driving. Driving flames remain.
- Removed flames from its garage preview; the lava body styling remains.

Map Maker
- The eight V2.4 props now use the very same model builders as the Playplace:
  seesaw, basketball, basketball hoop, soccer ball, cannon, foam cube,
  bowling ball and tunnel. Their textures, materials, dimensions and details
  come from the original Playplace models.
- Matching curved tunnel collisions, full-size hoop rim and backboard,
  seesaw pivot and cannon entry pad/launch position.
- Existing prop IDs and saved transforms remain. The larger original models
  can occupy more space than their V2.4 counterparts in existing creations.
  Use the editor's scale/move controls if a previous layout needs more room.

Compatibility
- Preserves V2.4 starters, car upgrades, bowling rewards, owner dashboard,
  original maps, and offline split-screen controls, garage and wallet rules.
- Global protocol 5 requires older clients to refresh before joining.
- Offline HTML is self-contained with no online features or music. The
  compact TXT is the same game for pasting into mobile HTML viewers.
- Restricted viewers may keep saves only until the preview is closed.
  For lasting saves, open the HTML directly in a browser that permits them.
