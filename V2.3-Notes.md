# ISLA V2.3

Adds signed-in leaderboards, optional profile ranks, complete NPC torsos/shirts and reliable handbrake drift rewards. The V2.24 vault and Iscoin balance remain intact; no music is included.

## Rankings

The main menu and community navigation have a Leaderboard button. Signed-out players see sign-in, and the server rejects anonymous leaderboard requests. After signing in from that screen, players return to the leaderboard.

- Top 100 and You & friends each have Aura, Iscoins and Stars tabs.
- Aura ranks lifetime earnings. Buying a car does not lower this ranking.
- Iscoins rank current balances. Stars count currently approved, published, visible maps, matching the existing profile count.
- Hidden Aura or Iscoins exclude the player from that category before either global or friend ranks are calculated. Other categories remain eligible.
- You & friends includes the viewer and accepted friends, not pending requests or follows. Blocked connections are excluded. Long friend lists paginate in groups of 100.
- Both global and friend ranks are shown, including a personal summary when the player falls outside the Top 100.
- Equal scores share a rank (1, 2, 2, 4). Names provide stable ordering within ties. Top 100 displays at most 100 players.
- Zero-star players show “Zero stars” and “—” for both ranks in the friends list and their summary. They do not fill the Top 100 Stars list.
- Profiles show a corner panel with visible Aura/Iscoin ranks and a Stars rank when they have stars. Customize profile includes “Show ranks on my profile,” enabled by default. Turning it off hides the panel without withdrawing from leaderboards. Hidden categories never appear in that panel, and ranks are not returned to signed-out viewers.

## Driving fixes

NPC shirts previously allocated their instance-color buffer when the draw count was zero, making torsos and sleeves disappear. The full color buffer is now allocated before rendering and remains available as NPCs enter and leave view.

A moving handbrake turn now qualifies even before lateral slip reaches the old threshold. Sustained drifting still requires two seconds and awards the existing 700–1,300 Aura range. Standing still, straight braking, water skims and airborne movement do not count as handbrake drifts.
