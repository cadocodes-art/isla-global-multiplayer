# ISLA V2.31

## Iscoins

- Spawn budgets increased by 50%: 180 coins on each main outdoor map (previously 120), and 90 in Play Place (previously 60). Approved custom maps target 180 where safe space permits.
- Retains 40% near roads and 60% elsewhere, safe spacing, and rare 10-Iscoin roof/water placements. Maps with insufficient road space can use safe off-road space.
- Personal pickup timers still respawn coins after three minutes. Upgrade prices and coin values are unchanged.
- Layout IDs change with this release so old pickup timers cannot hide coins at unrelated new locations.

## Flappy Car

All three levels now accelerate forward, brake into reverse, and steer their actual flight path while airborne, including after ramp jumps, ground flaps and Play Place launches. Steering works from rest and air controls remain available after the flap budget is exhausted. Upgraded steering and braking remain stronger.

Flaps still control upward lift and retain their existing budgets and landing refill. Nitro retains the selected level's speed limit. Ground driving and other cars' air abilities are preserved.

## Validation

- All 243 automated tests passed, including every Flappy upgrade level, turning from rest, reversing after ramp takeoff without flapping, controls with no remaining flaps, nitro limits and matching ramp/bounce controls in Play Place.
- Verified exact 180/90 coin totals and 40/60 road distribution on all five main maps, safe placement/spacing, client/server equality, approved custom-map layouts and personal respawn/reward behavior.
- World build, Cloudflare Worker dry-run and standalone offline build passed.
- The 19-file patch applies to the latest complete V2.3 GitHub upload (585617b); all 127 source and destination checksums passed. Existing deployment configuration is excluded from this patch.

Full 3D and iPad touch playtesting still need a device check. Nothing has been pushed or deployed by this update.
