# ISLA V2.2 — complete release notes

Prepared for the existing ISLA multiplayer repository. This source package has not been deployed to the live game.

## Iscoins

- Iscoins are used only to purchase car upgrade levels.
- Collect coins on the main maps, The Playplace and approved community maps. Unapproved community revisions do not award coins or activity Aura.
- Yellow-orange coins display **1** and award 1 Iscoin. Deeper orange/peach coins display **5** and award 5 Iscoins.
- The rarity setting interprets “25% as likely” as one 5-value spawn for every four 1-value spawns: approximately **80% / 20%**. Each map has a stable layout.
- Chosen balancing: up to 160 coins per main/community map and 80 in The Playplace; each player's collected coin respawns after three minutes. Pickups are personal.
- Balances appear in the garage, while driving, on the upgrade page and on account profiles. Hide the display through the Iscoin visibility setting, like Aura. Upgrade prices remain visible.
- Account balances persist on the server; guest balances persist in that browser. Purchase prices, duplicate pickups and account ownership are checked on the server.

## Garage upgrades

Every car starts at **Upgrade Level 1**, its original configuration. Owned cars have a purple **Upgrade** button immediately above **Drive**. The upgrade page shows the selected car, current level, unlocked level selectors and next upgrade.

The purchase preview occupies approximately 80% of the screen and shows the new appearance, stats, features and Iscoin price. Its purple button reads “Confirm Purchase for ___ Iscoins?”. Buy Level 2 before Level 3. Purchased levels remain unlocked and you can switch freely among them without paying again. Existing car ownership, Aura and paint are retained.

All 19 cars have distinct Level 2/3 visual details. Normal nitro speeds rise by approximately 12% at Level 2 and 25% over the original at Level 3, rounded to whole km/h. Upgrades also add car-specific abilities and handling or special-feature improvements. Level 3 retains Level 2 features unless its description replaces a limit. The complete price/stat/upgrade tables appear at the end of this document.

## Car behavior and controls

- **Nuker Level 1:** cannon points and shoots forward, with a 15-second interval.
- **Nuker Level 2:** adds a small cannon on each side, angled diagonally in opposite directions. All three fire every eight seconds. Its body is 10% larger and heavier, improving ramming. Costs 225 Iscoins.
- **Nuker Level 3:** keeps three cannons and auto-targets cars/buildings ahead. Cooldown is five seconds. An optional manual-fire setting enables **F / FIRE**, with the same cooldown. Costs 400 Iscoins.
- **Flappy Car:** angel-style feathered wings, about Solstice R's size, 268 km/h with normal nitro at Level 1, 705,000 Aura and 20 midair flaps per takeoff. Each **F / FLAP** press gives a small upward jump. Landing restores the flap budget. Levels 2/3 provide 24/28 flaps, stronger lift and air control.
- **Ordinary cars:** skim water for a randomly chosen six to seven seconds per entry. Their normal water-entry requirements remain.
- **Water Skidder:** at most 30 seconds of water travel, then a full ten seconds continuously on land to refill. Short land visits do not instantly reset its budget. It cannot cross map boundaries while on water. Upgrades retain these limits.
- **StickCar:** climbs walls indefinitely and transitions onto roofs. Drive across a roof and off any edge normally. Level 2 adds brake-to-hold; Level 3 adds sideways wall steering.
- **General steering:** turning is slightly easier.
- **Air tricks:** press **Space** once airborne, or double-tap the center of the mobile joystick. A flip or spin adds a single 3–4% bonus to that jump's landing Aura. Seven tricks still earn only one bonus.
- **Other abilities:** **F** or the labeled touch button activates DASH, LIFT, HOP, SURGE or CRUISE when equipped. Existing Ultra rockets retain **E / ROCKET**. Cruise cancels with the brake. Air brakes use the brake input.
- **Mobile joystick:** moved slightly up and right: 18 pixels normally, 14 right / 10 up in landscape. Touch handling prevents unwanted page zoom during controls. Physical Safari testing remains on the validation checklist.
- Players remain in their cars. Walking/getting out is not added back.

## New Aura activities

| Completed activity | Aura |
| --- | ---: |
| Launch an object with Launcher | 1,100–1,200 |
| Complete a controlled drift | 700–1,300 |
| Destroy a building using Nuker | 1,000–2,000 |
| Destroy a building by ramming | 1,200–1,300 |
| Playplace bowling spare | 1,200–1,400 |
| Playplace bowling strike | 3,000–4,000 |
| Complete a Playplace waterslide | 600–800 |
| Destroy a building you personally destroyed before | 100–200 |

A building's large reward is **once per player, permanently**. Returning the next day, joining another session or switching destruction methods does not reset it. Another player gets their own first reward. Approved custom buildings retain their identity across republication when their object ID is retained. Guest history is local to that browser; signed-in history persists in the account database.

Drifts must last at least two seconds. Bowling rewards attach to the player completing the roll. Waterslides require progression from the top to the exit over at least two seconds. Duplicate account reward events cannot pay twice, and short activity cooldowns reduce repeated triggers. These checks supplement the existing client-authoritative driving architecture; this release does not claim fully server-simulated anti-cheat.

## Tire trails

A trail-color control sits next to the existing car paint control. Soft colored tire trails fade over approximately ten seconds. Handbrake sliding/skidding creates black tire streaks. Trail color is saved with the garage.

## People and themed buildings

- Outdoor NPCs stay within five meters of their assigned building footprint and keep off roads.
- NPCs are also visible through building interiors/windows.
- Hit an outdoor NPC and they ragdoll for roughly three seconds, then get up and resume walking.
- Restaurants, shops, police stations and clinics give main-map buildings themes. The Playplace receives three small themed buildings.
- Map Maker includes new themed building types alongside existing houses/restaurants, with normal placement, transformations, saving and approval behavior.
- Pedestrians are decorative and do not award Aura. Rendering uses a bounded nearby population for crowded maps.

## Multiplayer and saved progress

Upgrade levels, Flappy wing flaps and air tricks synchronize between players. Authenticated global-room cars/levels must be owned and unlocked. Nuke cooldowns and shared weapon effects run through the multiplayer server. Currency, upgrades, selected levels, trail settings and Iscoin visibility persist with accounts. Older clients should reload after deployment because the multiplayer protocol is updated.

## Cado_Codes testing credit

After deployment to the existing Worker/account database, the server adds **10,000 Iscoins and 5,000,000 Aura** once to the existing verified **Cado_Codes** account. The migration records the account ID and grant so it cannot repeat on restart. It adds to current balances and preserves owned cars and upgrades. Guests using that display name cannot claim it. No live account has been changed while preparing this ZIP.

## Deferred

Plane Car mountain-to-mountain Aura farming changes for Islayan Peaks remain deferred to the possible **V2.21** update. Joke trailer suggestions such as an 8,000-mph car, garage weather forecast or throwing cows are not implemented.
