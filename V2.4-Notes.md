ISLA V2.4 — RELEASE NOTES

New cars and starters
- First-time drivers choose one free car: Bahia Classic or The Big Truck.
  The unchosen car stays locked. Each normally costs 20,000 Aura.
  Existing garages, progress, maps and upgrades are preserved.
- Big Truck: 154 kph nitro at level 1, stronger building impacts.
- Inferno: 800,000 Aura; nitro 302 / 378 / 453 kph at levels 1 / 2 / 3.
  Heat lasts 20 / 25 / 30 seconds on water. It cools visibly, then sinks.
  Recharge on land takes 12 / 10 / 8 seconds. Its stone trail lasts about
  30 seconds and supports other drivers, including in global rooms.
  This counts as water skimming when rewards are eligible.
- Grappler: 750,000 Aura; nitro 279 / 349 / 419 kph. Press F or GRAPPLE
  to latch a structure ahead, steer to swing, then press again to release.
- F1: 900,000 Aura; nitro 550 / 688 / 825 kph. Original Formula-style model.

Map Maker and bowling
- Place, rotate, scale, color and copy seesaws, basketballs, hoops, soccer
  balls, cannons, foam cubes, bowling balls and tunnels. Moving toys sync
  in custom global rooms. Up to 128 moving toys per map (1,200 total objects).
- Bowling final-frame payouts: strike 10–15k; spare 6–8k; 9 pins 2–3k;
  8 pins 800–1,000; 7 pins 200–300 Aura. Payouts do not stack per frame.

Offline local multiplayer
- Two players share one device and map. Choose side-by-side or top/bottom.
- Both use the normal driving HUD, touch joysticks, Nitro/HB and ability
  controls, popups, upgrade levels and garage styling. Cars can collide.
- All five main maps and Map Maker maps work. One shared pause/map choice.
- Your Aura/Iscoin balances remain visible but never increase in local
  multiplayer. Only owned cars/upgrades can be selected; no purchases.
- Player 1: WASD, left Shift nitro, Space HB, F ability, E rockets,
  R recover, C camera. Player 2: arrows, right Shift nitro, Enter HB,
  / ability, . rockets, Backspace recover, ] camera. G opens the garage.
- No accounts, Search, global rooms, sharing or music in the offline file.
  Local saves retain the previous offline storage namespace.

Online owner dashboard
- Open your profile, then Owner Dashboard. Stats, Accounts, Maps, Comments
  and Moderation history are inside, above the existing reports section.
- Player graphs: last hour, 24 hours, 7 days and 30 days; current, average,
  peak, player-hours, busiest weekdays, account/map/comment/plays totals.
- Tracks active online driving (solo and global), including guests.
  Signed-in accounts count once across devices. Offline files are excluded.
  Disconnects age out after 75 seconds. Graphs refresh every 30 seconds.
  Graph timestamps use your device timezone; weekday comparisons use UTC.
- History starts when V2.4 is first used online; no old player-count
  history is invented. Minute activity history is kept for 30 days.
- Search accounts, confirm exact name and map count, then permanently
  delete that account and its maps. The current owner cannot delete itself.
  Sessions/recovery are revoked; other users' shared histories are retained.
- Search and delete any map comment. Hide/restore published maps.
- Edit any published map in Map Maker, with explicit Publish edits.
  Creator credit and their separate draft stay intact. A changed revision
  prevents stale edits overwriting a newer upload. Undo the latest owner
  edit within seven days. Existing global players refresh for the new revision.
- Owner checks run on the server, not only in the interface. Approval,
  map/comment/account actions are recorded in Moderation history.

Preserved V2.38 content
- Five main maps, existing car levels and features, Maker tools, map plays,
  curved bowling, online vault music, and the offline owner tools remain.
- Mount Inferno and walking remain inactive. Inferno here is the new CAR.
- Global protocol 4 asks older clients to refresh before joining V2.4.
