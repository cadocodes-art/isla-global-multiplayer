# ISLA V2.32

1- and 5-Iscoin coins now use random positions across safe areas of each map. There is no fixed percentage reserved for roads or off-road areas. The proportion near roads naturally depends on the map's layout. Coins placed on raised roads use the road surface height.

- Keeps V2.31's increased totals: 180 coins per main outdoor map, 90 in Play Place, and up to 180 on approved custom maps where safe space permits.
- Regular coins still have an 80% chance of being worth 1 and a 20% chance of being worth 5.
- Rare 10-Iscoin coins still favor roofs and water, with ground fallback on dry maps without buildings.
- Preserves spacing, map boundaries and building-interior checks. Regular coins avoid open water and steep surface changes.
- Layout generation stays deterministic so the client and server agree. Pickup cooldowns remain personal and last three minutes; this does not introduce a fresh reroll on each visit.
- New layout IDs prevent previous-layout pickup timers from hiding unrelated coins.
- Keeps V2.31's Flappy Car fixes and all previous features, balances and prices.
