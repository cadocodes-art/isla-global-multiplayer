# ISLA V2.24 — Iscoins and the vault

V2.24 adds the hidden account vault, raises upgrade prices by 25% (rounded up to a whole Iscoin), reduces coin counts by 25%, and spreads pickups between roads and the rest of each map. Purchased upgrades and existing balances are retained. The soundtrack is excluded.

## Coin balance

- Main maps target 120 coins, down from 160. The Playplace targets 60, down from 80.
- 40% are near roads (within 12 meters of a road edge), and 60% elsewhere. Small or unusual approved maps use safe available space if that split cannot be filled.
- Coins are at least 32 meters apart, increased from 24.
- Rare purple coins marked 10 are worth 10 Iscoins. Main maps target two; The Playplace targets one (about 2% of pickups). They favor rooftops and water. Dry maps without suitable buildings can place them on land.
- Other coins retain the existing 1:5 denomination ratio: about one fifth are worth 5.
- The layout is deterministic; collection and the existing three-minute respawn remain personal to each player, including global rooms.
- The new layout has new coin IDs, so old pickup cooldowns do not hide unrelated new placements.

## One hidden vault

Find a textless circular button at 40% opacity at the very bottom of the driving pause menu. Entry requires 1,000,000 lifetime Aura; it never spends Aura. Below the threshold, the error shows the amount still needed. Eligible accounts see green “Access granted.” before the passcode screen fades in.

Rewards require a signed-in account, are checked on the server, and each passcode is redeemable once per account across devices and sessions. Letter case and surrounding spaces are ignored; the spaces and underscores within a passcode matter. Invalid or repeated codes award nothing. Earned Aura rewards increase both the wallet and lifetime Aura. Claim history is separate from garage imports.

## Verification

- All 228 automated checks passed, including all five map layouts, old-save compatibility, every upgrade price, all 15 vault rewards, repeat/concurrent claims, account separation, server restart and second-device sign-in.
- Cloudflare Worker dry-run, world generation and the offline single-file build passed.
- Desktop and 390-pixel phone vault layouts were inspected using the actual pause/vault HTML, CSS and JavaScript with simulated account responses. Locked, successful, duplicate and invalid passcodes displayed correctly; Escape returned to the pause menu.
- Server reward and persistence behavior was separately tested against the local Worker with real test accounts.
- The circle is 40% opacity at rest and on hover. Keyboard focus makes it more visible for accessibility.
- Full 3D play and physical iPad/Safari interaction were not verified in this environment. Test those before considering the release fully exercised on devices.
- The upload patch is checked against both the original complete V2.23 repository and the later soundtrack upload. The soundtrack is excluded from entry points, the source manifest, offline packaging and Cloudflare assets.
