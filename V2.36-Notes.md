# ISLA V2.36

- Removed the restaurant, shop and police station from Play Place. Its playground attractions remain.
- Play Place has 100 personal Iscoin slots per visit. Other main maps keep 240.
- Coins now use actual sky bridge, elevated road and water slide heights. Pool coins float above the water rather than on the pool bottom.
- Each visit includes coins in the sky bridge and water park areas, with a fresh random layout. Personal pickup cooldowns and coin values are retained.
- Trampoline-generated jump Aura is 20% of the former reward, including chain and trick multipliers. Bounce strength and ordinary jumps are unchanged.
- Updated the Play Place room revision so older clients are prompted to refresh.
- Includes the previous nearest-land water recovery, NPC skin colors, plateau and roof fixes. Existing saves, offline Owner Controls and History are retained.

This update is a cumulative patch over the complete V2.33 upload. The accompanying offline text file embeds all game code and assets. Neither package has been published to GitHub or Cloudflare.
