# ISLA V2.37

- StickCar rooftop jumps now award one quarter of their previous landing Aura, including chain and air-trick bonuses. The reduction follows the jump from takeoff, so landing on the ground, water, another roof or another building side all uses the same 25% rate. Applies at every upgrade level, online and offline. Other jump rewards are unchanged.

- Costa Metro keeps about three times its original overall population, now spread evenly across walkable land throughout the map. Each building has one indoor resident; outdoor pedestrians use separate, spaced routes across the neighborhoods, open areas and full park grounds. Routes avoid roads, buildings, fountains and tree trunks. The same three-second ragdoll/recovery behavior remains. Six instanced mesh batches render at most 216 nearby people; other maps retain their original population. Applies online and offline.

- Removed the oversized NPC-viewing facade openings. Only existing ground-floor window panes are transparent, with matching openings behind the glass. Doors, surrounding walls, painted frames and crossbars are preserved. Indoor people walk behind the real windows. Applies to the coastal homes, city houses and storefronts, Islayan Peak chalets, Fern Valley and Map Maker buildings, in both editions.

- Scoring a basket in Play Place gives 7,000–9,000 Aura. Credit follows the driver who last pushed the scoring ball. A ball passing through again without another push does not award a second reward. In global rooms only the scoring driver receives the reward event.
- Offline Map Maker maps are practice only: jumps, skims, drifting, destruction and other activities do not add Aura, and Iscoins do not spawn. The five built-in maps retain rewards. Existing balances and Owner Controls are preserved.
- The ability dock, including Flap and Rocket, moves 40 pixels left and 40 pixels up.
- Bowling balls weigh half as much. Their weight now affects launch speed and the minimum nudge needed to roll, so they are easier to push.
- Fixed solo Santa Luz and Santa Ruiz palm trees rendering black. Every tree instance starts with its neutral tint; wetting one tree preserves the rest.
- Updated the Play Place room revision for shared sports state.

Includes the previous V2.34–V2.36 terrain, nearest-land recovery and Play Place changes. This is a cumulative patch for a complete V2.33 or later repository. It has not been uploaded to GitHub or deployed to Cloudflare.
