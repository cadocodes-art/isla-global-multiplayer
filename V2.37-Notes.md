# ISLA V2.37

- Scoring a basket in Play Place gives 7,000–9,000 Aura. Credit follows the driver who last pushed the scoring ball. A ball passing through again without another push does not award a second reward. In global rooms only the scoring driver receives the reward event.
- Offline Map Maker maps are practice only: jumps, skims, drifting, destruction and other activities do not add Aura, and Iscoins do not spawn. The five built-in maps retain rewards. Existing balances and Owner Controls are preserved.
- The ability dock, including Flap and Rocket, moves 40 pixels left and 40 pixels up.
- Bowling balls weigh half as much. Their weight now affects launch speed and the minimum nudge needed to roll, so they are easier to push.
- Fixed solo Santa Luz and Santa Ruiz palm trees rendering black. Every tree instance starts with its neutral tint; wetting one tree preserves the rest.
- Updated the Play Place room revision for shared sports state.

Includes the previous V2.34–V2.36 terrain, nearest-land recovery and Play Place changes. This is a cumulative patch for a complete V2.33 or later repository. It has not been uploaded to GitHub or deployed to Cloudflare.
