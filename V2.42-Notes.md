ISLA V2.42

Inferno stone rendering fix
- Stone trails now start with a gray-brown base material, and their color
  buffer is allocated before the first frame, even when there are no stones.
  Previously the renderer could compile a white trail before the color data
  existed and keep using it when the first stones appeared.
- Verified an empty first frame followed by forged stones in real WebGL:
  the sampled lit stone pixel was RGB 163, 152, 132 (gray-brown, not white).
- Shared support, heat duration, 30-second lifetime and sinking are unchanged.

Grappler full-building orbits
- The orbit is centered on the building, rather than a point on its wall.
- Its minimum radius clears the building's furthest corner plus the car.
  If you attach near a wall, the Grappler moves outward before turning.
- Much faster, sustained orbiting: roughly a two-second lap around a small
  house once up to speed. Larger buildings take longer to circle.
- Left/right selects orbit direction. Brake or handbrake slows/stops it.
  Accelerate tightens the orbit within the safe radius. Nitro adds speed
  where the radius allows it. F / GRAPPLE releases with your momentum.
- The rope follows the near face as you travel around building corners.
- Nearest-building targeting, upgrade reach, car price and road speed remain.
- Other nearby buildings and obstacles still have their usual collisions.

Preserved content
- V2.41 original Playplace models in the editor, Inferno driving flames,
  ring removal and garage preview styling remain.
- V2.4 features, five main maps, accounts, owner dashboard, existing garages,
  offline split-screen and no-earnings/no-purchases rules are preserved.
- Existing global protocol 5 remains compatible. No database migration.

Checks
- Full repeated orbits around rotated rectangles at 30, 60 and 120 Hz.
- Real driving-physics test: at least two laps in six seconds around each
  tested building, with no impacts against the anchor building's corners.
- Steering reversal, braking, release momentum and independent local drivers.
- Stone initialization and GPU rendering after an empty first frame.
- Compact offline viewer startup. No direct iPad hardware test performed.
