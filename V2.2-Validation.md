This update is prepared for manual deployment to the existing ISLA Worker. No production deployment or live account write was performed.

## Automated checks

**Final result: 209 tests passed; 0 failed, skipped or cancelled.**

- Every new Aura range, replay suppression, invalid activity rejection, local bowling completion and waterslide progression.
- Forward/triple Nuker projectiles, auto-aim and turret direction, manual fire and multiplayer five-second cooldown.
- Flappy's 20/24/28 flap limits and landing resets; network flap/air-trick synchronization.
- Water Skidder's 30-second budget, ten-second land recharge, recovery behavior and map boundaries; ordinary six-to-seven-second skims.
- Indefinite wall climbing, wall hold/traverse, roofs and driving off their edges.
- A single air-trick bonus after repeated tricks; dash, lift, hop, cruise and upgraded rocket behavior.
- Fire Truck roof hoses and Launcher interaction parameters.

The existing account, map-editor, collision, physics, terrain, traffic, multiplayer, social, mobile-input and previous-release regression suites also run.

The temporary UI fixtures and sample balances are excluded from the ZIP and deployable assets.

## Limits and next device check

The available browser could not create a WebGL context. Therefore full 3D rendering and live driving were not visually verified there. Geometry, movement, interactions and network behavior were tested programmatically with the real modules. A mobile-width browser check does not replace physical iPhone/iPad Safari testing.

After deploying, make a short pass in a browser/device with working WebGL:

1. Sign in as Cado_Codes and confirm the additional 10,000 Iscoins / 5,000,000 Aura; reload and confirm they do not repeat.
2. Collect both coin values, purchase/switch upgrade levels, reload and confirm saved balances/levels.
3. Drive Flappy, Nuker, Water Skidder and StickCar; inspect wings, auto-aim, water timing and rooftop transitions.
4. Check themed windows/NPCs, colored fading trails and black handbrake streaks.
5. Test a bowling strike/spare, waterslide, drift, object launch and repeated building destruction.
6. Use two signed-in players to inspect upgrade visuals, shared projectiles, Flappy flaps and first-building rewards.
7. On Safari, double-tap the joystick center for air tricks and confirm the page does not zoom.

The existing architecture trusts client movement/activity reports in several places. Ownership checks, deduplication and cooldowns improve consistency; they are not a claim of complete anti-cheat. The separate Plane Car Aura-farming change remains deferred to V2.21.
