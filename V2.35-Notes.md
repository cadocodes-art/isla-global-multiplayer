# ISLA V2.35 — nearest land respawn revision

Sinking and pressing Recover while in water now use the nearest safe dry terrain near the car, with space for its chassis and clearance from solid objects. The recovered shore replaces the previous checkpoint. This removes the sink-to-old-road shortcut after mountain flights. Jump scoring, dry-land Recover, start positions, upgrade budgets and balances are unchanged. Custom maps with no safe land fall back to their authored start.

People now use ten skin tones ranging from light to deep brown. Each person's face and hands match, including indoor and outdoor NPCs. Colors remain stable as people come into view; the existing six instanced draw batches are retained.

The Islayan Peak plateau moves to the former southeast mountain site, between East Valley Road and South Shore Road, more than 2 km from the remaining northern mountain. Its broad flat top is 111 m high, approximately 18% of the 616 m summit. The previous southern plateau is removed. The town, roads, map boundary, taller mountain and corrected chalet roofs remain.

Online and offline editions contain both changes. Existing accounts and offline save namespaces are retained; offline Owner Controls and dated History remain available. Multiplayer uses terrain revision 5 so clients with older terrain must refresh.

Extract ISLA-V2.35-NEAREST-LAND-RESPAWN-ONLINE.zip, open the folder with that same name, and upload its 25 individual files to the repository root in one batch, replacing matches. Keep all other files and the existing Cloudflare configuration. This cumulative patch includes the V2.34 changes and works over a complete V2.33, V2.34 or V2.35 repository. It has not been pushed or deployed.

Validation: the production build and local Worker bundle passed. Terrain, roof geometry, NPC colors, road clearance, multiplayer and offline-save checks passed. Online and offline terrain matched at 9,409 sample positions. The compact offline file recovers the complete game exactly. Live browser 3D play-testing was unavailable.

Water recovery validation: seven targeted tests cover sinking, manual water recovery, checkpoint replacement, nearby islands, cliffs and obstacles, Islayan Peak, Play Place pools, tiny hand-made islands, custom-map recovery and ordinary resets. The existing water regression and 39 driving/upgrade/custom-road checks also passed.
