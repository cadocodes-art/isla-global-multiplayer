# ISLA V2.21

## Repository audit

Checked `cadocodes-art/isla-global-multiplayer`, main commit `788bd53`. Every one of the 112 source files listed in the V2.2 upload manifest exists and has the expected SHA-256 checksum. No required V2.2 source was missing or altered.

## Fixes

- Coin geometry remains vertical and cap texture coordinates keep the number upright on both faces throughout its rotation.
- The driving Iscoin balance is part of the Aura panel's normal layout, below its existing content. The visibility preference still works.
- Flappy can take off from dry ground or a roof with F / FLAP. Takeoff consumes the first flap of the 20/24/28 budget. Landing replenishes the budget as before. Water/sinking and cannon states do not allow ground takeoff.
- The ability button responds to pointer-down, including a non-primary finger while the joystick is captured. Default touch gestures are suppressed without releasing the joystick. A subsequent generated click does not spend another flap; keyboard activation still works.
- Shared ability layout places F abilities and Ultra's rocket button above the speed/RPM/nitro display, instead of at fixed overlapping screen coordinates. Hidden and paused buttons stay hidden.
- The joystick now moves 42 pixels right/up from its original position, or 30 right / 28 up in short landscape layouts. The minimap is repositioned so it clears both the joystick and taller Aura/Iscoin panel.
- Air-trick rotation is applied to a complete-car group containing the body, wheels and accessories for local and remote vehicles. The ground contact shadow remains separate. Normal steering and wheel spin continue inside that group.
- Menu, community header, package and health endpoint report V2.21.
