# ISLA V2.34 — Islayan Peak

Islayan Peaks is now Islayan Peak: one northern mountain in the former tallest summit’s location, about 20% higher. The other four mountains are replaced by low snowy terrain. The 4.8 km playable boundary, coastline, central village, and connected roads remain.

This revised V2.34 corrects the chalet roofs' reversed faces and adds a broad, flat snowy plateau on the southern side of the map, opposite the northern mountain. The plateau is 154 m high, about 25% of the 616 m summit. The misplaced northern plateau is removed, and the existing roads retain their gentle grades. All 131 village plots are retained.

The internal map ID stays `peaks`, preserving bookmarks and personal coin cooldowns. The multiplayer room uses a new terrain revision; older clients must refresh before joining. Existing account balances, cars and upgrades are retained.

Offline: includes the same remake plus the previously added Owner Controls and dated History. The password verifier and existing offline storage keys are retained. Offline controls are not included in the online update.
